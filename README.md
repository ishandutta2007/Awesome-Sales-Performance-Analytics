# Awesome-Sales-Performance-Analytics

## Top Sales Performance Analytics Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Pipeline Intelligence, Forecast Accuracy & Self-Hosted Sales Analytics*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial sales performance analytics platforms** and **open-source projects** that analyze pipeline health, forecast revenue, measure rep performance, and surface deal risks — from AI-powered conversation intelligence to self-hosted BI dashboards built on CRM data.



**Examples** include Salesforce Sales Analytics, Tableau, Power BI, InsightSquared, Gong Forecast, Clari, Looker, Aviso, Mediafly, and Atrium HQ (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Sales performance analytics is a growing open-source domain. **OpenCRM** provides a self-hosted CRM foundation that can feed analytics pipelines . **Metabase**, **Apache Superset**, and **Redash** deliver SQL-native BI dashboards for sales data without per-seat licensing . **Streamlit** and **Evidence.dev** enable code-first sales analytics apps . **DuckDB** and **ClickHouse** power analytical backends on CRM exports . **Jitsu** handles event pipeline ingestion from product and CRM systems . **SampleFlow** brings auditable sales performance ledgers with immutable event chains . This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



### BI & Dashboard Platforms



- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**  

  **The leading open-source modern data exploration and visualization platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **60,000+ GitHub stars** . **Rich visualization library with SQL IDE, semantic layer, and advanced analytics** . **Superset MCP server** enables LLM access to datasets, charts, and dashboards . **Best for open-source sales BI dashboards** .



- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  

  **Open-source BI and analytics**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **40,000+ GitHub stars** . **No-code question builder** for sales reps and managers . **Dashboards, alerts, and subscriptions** . **Best for self-service sales analytics** .



- **[Redash](https://github.com/getredash/redash)**  

  **Open-source data visualization and dashboarding**, BSD-2-Clause licensed with **25,000+ GitHub stars** . **SQL-based query editor** with visualization and dashboards . **Best for SQL-driven sales dashboards** .



- **[Grafana](https://github.com/grafana/grafana)**  

  **The de facto standard for open-source dashboards**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **65,000+ GitHub stars** . **Connects to 100+ data sources** . **Best for operational sales dashboards** .



### Code-First Analytics



- **[Streamlit](https://github.com/streamlit/streamlit)**  

  **Python framework for building data apps**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **35,000+ GitHub stars** . **Turn Python scripts into interactive sales dashboards** . **Best for custom sales analytics apps** .



- **[Evidence.dev](https://github.com/evidence-dev/evidence)**  

  **Code-based BI platform**, MIT licensed with **4,000+ GitHub stars** . **Markdown + SQL** — build sales dashboards as code . **Best for developer-centric sales analytics** .



- **[Lightdash](https://github.com/lightdash/lightdash)**  

  **Open-source Looker alternative for dbt users**, MIT licensed with **4,000+ GitHub stars** . **Semantic layer built on dbt** . **Best for dbt-centric sales analytics** .



- **[Cube](https://github.com/cube-js/cube)**  

  **Open-source semantic layer for data applications**, MIT licensed with **17,000+ GitHub stars** . **Headless BI** — define sales metrics once . **Best for consistent sales metrics** .



### Analytical Backends



- **[ClickHouse](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse)**  

  **The leading columnar analytical database**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **35,000+ GitHub stars** . **Real-time ingestion and sub-second queries** . **Best for large-scale sales analytics** .



- **[DuckDB](https://github.com/duckdb/duckdb)**  

  **In-process analytical database**, MIT licensed with **20,000+ GitHub stars** . **"SQLite for analytics"** — query CRM exports locally . **Best for embedded sales analytics** .



- **[Apache Doris](https://github.com/apache/doris)**  

  **Real-time analytical database**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **12,000+ GitHub stars** . **High-performance SQL analytics** . **Best for real-time sales analytics** .



- **[StarRocks](https://github.com/StarRocks/starrocks)**  

  **High-performance analytical database**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **8,000+ GitHub stars** . **Real-time analytics with lakehouse integration** . **Best for modern sales analytics** .



### CRM & Performance Foundations



- **[OpenCRM](https://github.com/OpenCRM-Project/OpenCRM)**  

  **Self-hosted CRM foundation** — contacts, accounts, opportunities, and pipeline data that can feed analytics pipelines . **Best for self-hosted CRM with analytics potential** .



- **[SampleFlow](https://github.com/Eclipseic1848/SampleFlow)**  

  **Sales performance and target management web system**, open-source . **Immutable performance event chain** — append-only, never overwritten performance ledger . **Layered target assignment** with approval workflows . **Controlled Excel import** with pre-check and rollback . **Best for auditable sales performance analytics** .



- **[Jitsu](https://github.com/jitsucom/jitsu)**  

  **Open-source data collection platform**, MIT licensed with **3,500+ GitHub stars** . **Collect event data and send to any destination** . **Segment alternative** . **Best for event data pipelines feeding sales analytics** .

**Frameworks for building custom sales performance analytics solutions**: Combine **Apache Superset** or **Metabase** for self-service sales dashboards . Use **Streamlit** or **Evidence.dev** for code-first sales analytics apps . Deploy **Lightdash** or **Cube** for dbt-centric semantic layers . Power with **ClickHouse** or **DuckDB** for analytical queries on CRM data . Integrate **Jitsu** for event pipeline ingestion . Use **SampleFlow** for auditable sales performance tracking . Note that true enterprise sales analytics with AI-powered forecast prediction, conversation intelligence, and vendor-supported SLAs (Gong Forecast, Clari, Aviso) remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong dashboarding, semantic modeling, and analytical backends that require integration for complete sales performance analytics.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Sales performance analytics platforms handle sensitive sales data and may process PII. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations.

- **Data quality determines analytics quality** — CRM data hygiene is critical. Poor data leads to misleading dashboards and forecasts. Implement validation pipelines with Great Expectations before building analytics .

- **Forecast accuracy is hard** — open-source stacks provide dashboards and models but lack the AI-powered conversation intelligence and prediction engines of commercial platforms .

- **License considerations**: Superset uses Apache-2.0 , Metabase uses AGPL-3.0 , Streamlit uses Apache-2.0 , Evidence.dev uses MIT , Lightdash uses MIT , and Cube uses MIT . Verify licensing against your use case before committing.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong dashboarding, semantic modeling, and analytical backends, but **AI-powered forecast prediction, conversation intelligence, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
